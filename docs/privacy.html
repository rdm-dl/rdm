Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Short version: RDM and the RDM browser extension do not collect, store, sell or send any personal data. Everything stays on your computer.

What the extension does

The RDM extension passes downloads and media links from your browser to the RDM app that runs on your own computer, using the browser's native messaging feature. To do this it reads:

  • the address, file name, size and type of downloads you start, and of video, audio and subtitle files on the page you are viewing;
  • the cookies, referrer and request headers that the website itself needs so RDM can download the file the same way the browser would.

This information is sent only to the RDM app on the same computer. It never goes to us or to any other server.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind, in the app or in the extension.

What is stored on your computer

The extension keeps its own settings (for example whether the video button is shown and the sites where you hid it) in the browser's extension storage. The RDM app keeps your download list and settings in its own data folder. You can remove both at any time by uninstalling the extension or deleting the app's data folder.

Network connections

RDM connects only to the servers of the files you choose to download, and to the websites whose pages you ask it to grab. It does not contact any server of ours.

Children

RDM does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
